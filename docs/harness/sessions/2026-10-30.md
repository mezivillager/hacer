## 1. The goal

A fix wave should not be the last unreviewed change on a branch, and a fix should not quietly change more than
the finding it closes. Every fix round now ends with the coordinator's three-part delta-read and then a fix-diff
delta review over the fix diff only, under a fix brief that bounds the diff, with one fix wave per review event
and a cause tag on every later finding, so a run's record shows the share of post-fix findings a fix wave
introduced (#1078).
