## 3. What recurred, and what caught it

  per bit. A real `Not` self-loop through a joiner and a splitter imported as `Not(in=false)` and
  compiled to `out=1`. Of 749 real cycles the verifier generated, 216 compiled, and the same cause gave
  wrong values on 51 of 1,062 and 220 of 1,471 R837-class documents. R837 held. The builder fixed it
  red first (4 of 62 failing) with a per-bit window guard on bus pins and junctions, and widened the
  corpus to 2–16-bit buses and rings: the old guard fails 19 of 200 documents and the fix 0.
